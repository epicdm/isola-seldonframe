## Supported integration contract the connector should target (Chatwoot 4.18.0 public API/webhooks; no live use now)
Agent-bot conversation ownership: bot conversations are `pending`; handoff to a human opens the conversation with an assignee/team; resolving returns it to the bot only through the agreed handback action. Webhooks are account-level, top-level payloads (no nested `conversation` key for some events), delivered at least once and not guaranteed ordered: dedupe on event/message id and reconcile by reading the API. Agent-bot tokens cannot list messages and `source_id` is not unique: key messages by (account_id, message_id). Details and the Uplink-side state machine: design note 03 (r1 Port entity). Connector credentials, when authorized, are entered by the owner in the Uplink app environment in EasyPanel; agents stay value-blind.
